# 👋 Welcome to Tydora

This is a blank page. 📄

The words you are reading right now — and every word you will ever write here — live quietly on your own computer as plain Markdown files. No uploads, no lock-in. They will still be here in ten years. 🔒

Writing should be simple: an idea 💡, a pen ✒️, and a page that never rushes you. That is exactly the page Tydora wants to be.

## ✍️ Two ways to write

Press `Ctrl+/` and feel the difference:

- **Live Render mode** ✨ —— what you see now. **Bold**, *italic*, `code`, ==highlight== — all at your fingertips, WYSIWYG.
- **Source mode** ⌨️ —— the same text, bare. Pure Markdown characters, for those who love to type symbols.

Switch any time — your words never get lost.

## 🌱 A few things worth trying

- 🔗 Use links like `[[Welcome]]` to connect ideas into a web, then watch them take shape in the graph view.
- 🧠 Press `Ctrl+M` to turn a note into a mind map.
- 🧩 Drag cards onto the canvas and rearrange your inspirations on an endless whiteboard.
- 🔍 Press `Ctrl+P` for the command palette — whenever you forget where a feature lives, it is always there.
- 💻 Press `Ctrl+\`` to open a terminal; writing and the command line are one backtick apart.

## 📚 Going deeper

- ⌨️ Keyboard-first? Take a look at [[Shortcuts]].
- 🎨 Themes, fonts, typewriter mode? Press `Ctrl+,` — it is all in Settings.

That's all.

Now, delete this text — or keep it.

Then, write your first line. 🚀
